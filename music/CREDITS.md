# Boomtown USA music credits

All tracks are CC0 (public domain dedication) from OpenGameArt.org, converted to AAC (.m4a) with afconvert so Safari can play them.

| File | Where it plays | Title | Author | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| forest-8bit.m4a | home, job board, town, shop | 8-bit Forest Theme | Wolfgang_ | https://opengameart.org/content/8-bit-forest-theme |
| mountain-stage.m4a | work rounds (silent during reading) | Mountain Stage | MintoDog | https://opengameart.org/content/mountain-stage |
| action-1.m4a | break games | 5 Chiptunes (Action), Level 1 | Juhani Junkala (SubspaceAudio) | https://opengameart.org/content/5-chiptunes-action |

Sound effects are synthesized in `js/sfx.js`. Voice lines in `voice/` were generated with ElevenLabs (see `voice/README.md`).
